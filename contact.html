Need Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your visit, reservations, and salon guidelines.

01.

Do I need an advance appointment?

While we happily welcome walk-ins based on daily chair availability, we strongly recommend booking 24–48 hours ahead to secure your preferred artist and time slot.

02.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand plans change. We kindly request at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling without any cancellation fee.

03.

Are consultation sessions complimentary?

Yes! All bridal, high-lift hair coloring, and advanced facial peel consultations are 100% complimentary prior to beginning any service.

04.

Is there parking available on premises?

Yes, complimentary valet and secure on-site reserved parking is available right behind our 500 Park Avenue salon for all guests.
