Hair Care Tips

Expert advice from our stylists — simple habits that keep your hair healthy between salon visits.

Wash Less, Rinse More

Shampoo 2–3 times per week to preserve natural oils. In between, a cool water rinse is enough to refresh hair without stripping it.

Air-Dry When You Can

Heat styling is the #1 cause of damage. Let hair dry naturally 50% before using a blow-dryer, and always use heat protectant.

Deep Condition Weekly

Once a week, swap your regular conditioner for a deep mask. Leave it on for 15–20 minutes for maximum absorption.

Trim Every 6–8 Weeks

Regular trims prevent split ends from travelling up the strand. Even if you're growing hair long, trims keep it healthy.

Protect from Sun & Chlorine

UV rays fade colour and dry out strands. Before swimming or sun exposure, apply leave-in oil or wear a hat.

Feed Your Hair From Within

Protein, iron, biotin, and omega-3s support healthy hair growth. A balanced diet shows in your hair within 3 months.
