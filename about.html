About Our Salon

Where Every Detail Matters

Glamora was born from a simple belief — that beauty should feel personal. In 2010, founder Isabella Rossi opened the doors of our first studio with three chairs, a handful of premium products, and a vision to create a salon that felt like home.

Today, our flagship location spans over 5,000 square feet of thoughtfully designed space. Every corner — from our serene treatment rooms to our state-of-the-art styling stations — has been crafted to make you feel calm, cared for, and completely yourself.

We believe in quality over quantity. That's why every service is performed by a certified expert, and every product we use is carefully vetted for efficacy, safety, and ethical sourcing. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just beautiful results.
