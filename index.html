About Us

A Sanctuary of Style & Care

Founded in 2010, Glamora began as a small studio with a big vision — to create a space where beauty is personal, unhurried, and always exceptional.

Today, our team of master stylists, estheticians, and makeup artists brings that same passion to every client. We use only premium, cruelty-free products, and we tailor every treatment to your unique features, lifestyle, and goals.
