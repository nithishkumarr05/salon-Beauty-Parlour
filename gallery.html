Behind the Scenes

Our Salon Moments & Philosophy

An inside perspective into our uncompromising standards of care — from diagnostic hair analysis and clinical hygiene to ethical alchemy and master craftsmanship.

Philosophy 01

Diagnostic Consultations

Before scissors touch a single strand or serum touches skin, every appointment begins with a thorough 1-on-1 dialogue. We assess facial bone structure, skin undertones, lifestyle demands, and hair porosity to prescribe a truly personalized routine.

Bespoke Face & Scalp Profiling
Philosophy 02

Clean & Ethical Alchemy

We firmly reject harsh sulfates, ammonia fumes, and micro-plastics. Every formula used at Glamora is certified cruelty-free, bio-compatible, and ethically sourced from eco-certified laboratories in France, Italy, and Japan.

100% Non-Toxic & Cruelty-Free
Philosophy 03

Clinical Hygiene Protocol

Immaculate sanitation is fundamental to luxury care. All steel instruments undergo triple-cycle ultrasonic cleaning followed by hospital-grade dry autoclave sterilization sealed in single-use surgical pouches.

Autoclave Sterilized & Sealed Tools
Philosophy 04

200+ Hours Annual Training

Trends evolve constantly; our techniques stay ahead of them. Our artists and therapists complete mandatory bi-annual masterclasses in London and Milan covering precision French cutting, Korean glass-skin hydra-facials, and airbrush mastery.

Global Master Stylist Certifications
Philosophy 05

Multisensory Serenity

From custom ambient playlist soundscapes to diffused natural lavender and cedarwood botanical mists, every salon suite is designed as a peaceful retreat away from urban noise and hurried schedules.

Aromatherapeutic Micro-Climate
Philosophy 06

Home Longevity Blueprint

The Glamora experience doesn't end at checkout. Every client departs with a handwritten step-by-step home maintenance guide and curated sample regimen to sustain salon-fresh results for months between appointments.

Comprehensive Take-Home Protocol
