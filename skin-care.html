The Esthetician Blueprint

Skin Care Golden Rules

Science-backed daily rituals prescribed by our master estheticians — ensuring glass-like skin longevity between clinic appointments.

AM
Morning Defense Phase

Protect, Hydrate & Shield

Gentle pH-balanced cleanser → L-ascorbic acid Vitamin C antioxidant serum → ceramide barrier cream → SPF 50+ mineral filter. UV exposure causes 80% of preventable premature aging.

PM
Night Cellular Repair Phase

Double Cleanse & Cellular Resurfacing

Hydrophilic cleansing balm → foaming amino cleanser → bio-retinoid or peptide concentrate → lipid recovery night elixir. Your skin carries out 90% of cellular mitotic division while sleeping.

2x
Weekly Exfoliation Window

Bio-Enzymatic Resurfacing

Gentle enzymatic exfoliation twice weekly dissolves dead corneocyte keratin plugs, unblocks micro-pores, and boosts transdermal serum delivery by over 40%.

RULE 01 Cellular Water

Hydrate from the Deep Core

Consume 2.5+ liters of electrolyte-balanced water daily. Deep dermis is 70% water — systemic cellular hydration reveals natural dewiness long before topical creams can penetrate.

RULE 02 Broad Spectrum

Non-Negotiable Daily SPF 50

Apply high-purity broad-spectrum sunscreen 365 days a year, even on overcast mornings and indoors near windows. UV-A rays penetrate clouds and glass, degrading collagen cross-links continuously.

RULE 03 Friction Defense

Sleep on Pure Mulberry Silk

High-grade 22-momme silk pillowcases reduce facial skin friction by 43% and absorb significantly less topical skincare than cotton, preventing sleep creases and dry overnight dehydration.

RULE 04 Bio-Lipids

Feed Your Cellular Membrane

Incorporate rich Omega-3 fatty acids, natural astaxanthin, and polyphenols from avocado, wild salmon, and walnuts to build a dense, resilient lipid layer against external inflammation.

RULE 05 Microbiome Guard

Respect the Skin Acid Mantle

Refrain from absent-minded face touching and never pick inflamed spots. Clean your smartphone glass daily with alcohol wipes to avoid transferring bacteria onto cheeks and jawline.

RULE 06 Clinical Care

Monthly Esthetician Maintenance

Epidermal cells regenerate completely every 28 days. A professional medical facial every 4 to 6 weeks clears stubborn micro-comedones and resets barrier health year-round.
